⚡ *1️⃣ PEHLA SHIELD: ODD vs EVEN RULE*
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• *M-Units (M1, M2)*: Hamesha **EVEN strikes** (`23400, 23600, 23800...`) lete hain.
• *OS1 System*: Hamesha **ODD strikes** (`23100, 23300, 23500, 23700, 23900...`) leta hai.
• Is niyam ki wajah se 95% trades waise hi aapas me kabhi nahi takrati.

📌 *Example Case*:
• M1 ne Sell kiya: **`23400 CE`**
• 500-point hedge bani: **`23900 CE`**
• Lekin `23900 CE` pehle se OS1 me chal rahi hai (Occupied hai).

📌 *Software ka Exact Action (Shift Rule)*:
1. Software order bhejne se pehle broker positions aur database check karta hai.
2. Jaise hi usko dikhta hai ki `23900 CE` busy hai:
   👉 **Software Hedge ko turant +200 Points DOOR shift kar deta hai!**
3. *New Hedge Strike*: `23900 CE + 200` = **`24100 CE`** par buy karega.
4. *PE Side me*: Agar `22700 PE` occupied hai, toh -200 point neeche **`22500 PE`** par shift kar dega.
