The Complete Operational Lifecycle

1

Step 1: Choose Horizon & Click "Fetch Relevant Strikes"

Operator chooses Weekly or Monthly expiry from dropdown. Clicks "Fetch Relevant Strike Price & Hedge Price". The engine scans 100-multiple strikes ≤ ₹150, queries yesterday's closing reference price, and determines which wing(s) are decaying.

2

Step 2: Anytime Execution with 1-Trade-Per-Day Guard

Operator can click execute at any time of day (morning, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, or evening). The system strictly enforces: Din mein sirf ek hi baar trade karna hai. If Call is decaying → Sells CE. If Put is decaying → Sells PE. If both decaying → Sells Both. Long hedge is verified and bought FIRST.

3

Step 3: Autonomous "Stopwatch" (1.382 Stop Loss)

Once entered, leave it running ("rang chhod dena hai"). The background daemon acts as a stopwatch monitoring the individual 1.382 Stop Loss trigger. If the premium expands to 1.382x entry, it auto-exits the short leg to protect capital. The protective orphan hedge stays open.

4

Step 4: 3:00 PM Line Decision & Next Day

At 15:00 IST daily: If the trade is still running below yesterday's line → CONTINUATION into Day 2, 3, 4! If the price cuts above the line → AUTO-CLOSE at market. Full manual override allows the operator to exit or adjust anytime.
