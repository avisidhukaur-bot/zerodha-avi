🛡️ 3-Step Automated Re-Entry Safety Flow

Step 1 • 5-Minute Post-Exit Cooldown (300s)

Jab SL hit hota hai, market me temporary spike hoti hai. Engine agle 5 minute tak re-entry scan ko freeze rakhta hai taaki badhte hue premium me dobara trade na trigger ho.

Step 2 • Price Threshold Condition

Re-entry tabhi scan hoti hai jab option premium wapas discount level par aa jaye:
LTP ≤ Anchor Price - ₹1.00 Buffer
Example: Anchor ₹39 hai toh LTP ₹38 ya uske neeche aana anivarya hai.

Step 3 • 5-Minute Continuous Hold Confirmation (300s)

LTP ₹38 ke neeche aane par system turant sell nahi karta. Price ko lagatar 5 minute (300 seconds) tak ₹38 ke neeche tikna padta hai. Agar beech me price upar spike maarta hai, toh confirmation timer wapas 0 ho jata hai. Jab continuous 5 min complete hote hain, tabhi order place hota hai.
