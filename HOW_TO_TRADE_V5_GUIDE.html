🎯 1. Yeh System Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Basic Funda)

Yeh system NIFTY Option Selling par chalta hai. Iska main kaam hai Time Decay (Theta) se profit kamana, yaani jaise-jaise samay beet-ta hai, option ka premium galta (zero hota) hai aur hume munafa hota hai.

💡 Sabse Bada Golden Rule:
Hum ek time par sirf ek hi direction me trade karte hain (ya toh sirf CALL bechte hain ya sirf PUT). Isse market chahe kitna bhi bhaage, dono side loss hone ka khatra 100% khatam ho jata hai!

🧭 2. Master Nifty Anchor (Market Ka Compass)

Master Anchor Nifty ka ek reference level hota hai (Jaise ₹24,000).

🟢 BULLISH (Tezi Ka Trend)

Jab Nifty Spot ≥ Master Anchor

Nifty upar ja raha hai, isliye hum sirf PUT (PE) Sell karenge aur Call ko Muted (Pending) rakhenge.

Execute: PE Sell + PE Hedge

🔴 BEARISH (Mandi Ka Trend)

Jab Nifty Spot < Master Anchor

Nifty neeche gir raha hai, isliye hum sirf CALL (CE) Sell karenge aur Put ko Muted (Pending) rakhenge.

Execute: CE Sell + CE Hedge

⚡ 3. Trade Entry & Auto LTP Rule

Pehle logo ko anchor price daalne me dikkat aati thi. Ab system me Auto-LTP Feature hai:

1
Auto LTP Detection: Jaise hi aap Strike chunte hain (jaise 24300 CE), system turant Kite se uska Live Market Price (LTP) nikaal leta hai.
2
Anchor Price = Current LTP: Agar aap koi price nahi daalte, toh system default me live market price ko hi Anchor bana leta hai.
3
Hedge First Rule (Margin Safety): Zerodha me margin kam lage isliye system hamesha pehle Hedge Buy order daalta hai, fir Sell order daalta hai.

🛑 4. Stop Loss Rule (25% Rule Example)

Har bechi hui leg ka apna independent 25% Stop Loss hota hai:

📝 Simple Example:
• Agar aapne Option becha (LTP / Entry): ₹100.00
• Stop Loss %: 25%
• SL Trigger Price = 100 + (100 × 25%) = ₹125.00

👉 Action: Agar market ulti chal padi aur option ka price badhkar ₹125 touch kiya, toh engine turant us bechi hui leg ko square-off (buy back) karke loss ko lock kar dega!
🛡️ Orphan Hedge Protection:
Jab Stop Loss hit hota hai, toh bechi hui leg close hoti hai lekin Hedge ko system kabhi close nahi karta! Hedge open rehta hai taaki agar market me bohot bada crash ya spike aaye toh hedge aapke capital ko 100% protect kare.

🕒 5. 3:00 PM Continuation Rule (Overnight Decision)

Rojana theek 15:00 IST (3:00 PM) par engine automatically evaluate karta hai:

Condition (Spot vs Anchor) Favorable Leg Counter Leg Action
Spot ≥ Master Anchor PUT (PE) Sell CALL (CE) Sell PE ko agale din ke liye hold karega (`CONTINUED`), CE ko close karega.
Spot < Master Anchor CALL (CE) Sell PUT (PE) Sell CE ko agale din ke liye hold karega (`CONTINUED`), PE ko close karega.
